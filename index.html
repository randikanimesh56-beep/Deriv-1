Method and limitations
• EMA 9/21, RSI 14, MACD 12/26/9 and ATR 14 use completed 1-minute candles.
• Live entry confirmation compares the current tick with a tick approximately 30 seconds earlier and checks its position relative to EMA 9.
• Backtest uses 15 one-minute bars for a 15-minute horizon. It does not reproduce broker execution or payout exactly.
• Confluence score is not a probability. Spread, payout, slippage, news and execution delays are not modelled. Demo-test before relying on signals.
